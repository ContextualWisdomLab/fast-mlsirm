# Rust 1.98.1 repository compiler baseline

## Changed

- Raised the reviewed repository build and scientific-study compiler baseline from Rust 1.97.1 to 1.98.1 while leaving published crate MSRV metadata unchanged.
- Bound every pinned CI and statistical-study `dtolnay/rust-toolchain` input to the exact compiler channel declared by `rust-toolchain.toml`, preventing a Dependabot manifest update from silently leaving workflow setup and reproducibility evidence on the predecessor compiler.
- Compiler-baseline pull requests that change `rust-toolchain.toml` now run the repository's release-mode statistical-study workflow on the pull-request head, so ignored-study execution, literature recovery, GRM Monte Carlo recovery, and GPU-versus-CPU recovery parity are current-head evidence rather than schedule/tag-only evidence.
- A synchronized compiler-baseline pull-request head now cancels an obsolete same-ref statistical-study run instead of serializing current-head Monte Carlo evidence behind the predecessor head. Scheduled/tag runs keep their own refs, while PR evidence converges on the latest exact source head without sample reduction or study omission.
- Split the testlet recovery Monte Carlo into independent normal-ability and skew-ability 500-replication studies and raised only their harness iteration budget from 1,500 to 1,800 after Rust 1.98.1 exposed a deterministic normal-condition replicate that had not yet reached the unchanged `1e-6` likelihood tolerance. The seeds, 500-replication denominator per condition, RMSE/bias thresholds, and 100% convergence requirement remain unchanged.
- Moved the unchanged correlated-MIRT 500-replication D=2/D=3 normal/skew recovery study out of the generic 7,200-second ignored-test shard budget into a dedicated bounded Actions job with a retained exact-head log artifact. The predecessor Rust 1.98.1 run completed both D=2 cells with 100% convergence before timing out while continuing into D=3; no replication count, sample size, quadrature rule, recovery threshold, model formula, or estimator tolerance was weakened to obtain evidence.
- Moved the unchanged QMC-MIRT 500-replication D=4/D=5 Halton recovery study out of the same generic 7,200-second ignored-test shard budget after exact Rust 1.98.1 evidence timed out inside that target. A dedicated bounded Actions job now owns the complete study and retains its exact-head log artifact; the 500-replication denominator, D=4/D=5 design, Halton point counts (4,000/6,000), sample sizes (2,000/1,500), estimator settings, and scientific acceptance criteria are unchanged.
- Moved the unchanged GPCM-MHRM 500-replication D=2/D=5 normal/skew recovery study out of the generic 7,200-second ignored-test shard budget after both D=2 cells completed with 100% convergence and the shard timed out while continuing the study. A dedicated 360-minute job now owns the full target and retains its log artifact; replication counts, dimensions, sample sizes, estimator settings, and scientific acceptance thresholds remain unchanged.
- Repaired the 2PL MHRM 500-replication D=6 fixture after exact-head execution exposed an all-zero loading-pattern row at item 18. The fixture now reserves only the final item for the existing negative cross-loader and assigns every preceding remainder item to a valid factor, preserving D=2/D=6, N=2,000/2,500, 500 replications per normal/skew condition, estimator settings, and acceptance thresholds. The unchanged full MHRM recovery target now runs in its own 360-minute retained-log evidence job rather than inheriting the generic child budget.
- Isolated the unchanged normal-ability and skew-ability testlet recovery cells from the generic 180-minute ignored-study shard after exact Rust 1.98.1 execution completed a 4,642.96-second GPCM recovery study plus other targets and was then cancelled while the skew testlet cell was still computing. Each testlet cell now owns an independent 360-minute retained-log job. N=1,000, 24 items in four testlets, `q_gamma=15`, 1,800 harness iterations, the `1e-6` estimator tolerance, 500 replications per condition, RMSE(beta) `<0.12`, RMSE(sigma2) `<0.15`, deterministic seeds, and the 100% convergence requirement are unchanged.
