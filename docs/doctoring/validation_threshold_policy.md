# Automated-scoring validation threshold policy

## Summary

High-stakes automated scoring acceptance uses conjunctive gates on quadratic
weighted kappa, Pearson correlation, degradation from a human–human baseline,
and standardized mean difference (overall and subgroup). Governance requires
every verdict to name an immutable policy identity and version, and custom
research thresholds must reach the Rust decision owner rather than being
re-applied only in Python.
